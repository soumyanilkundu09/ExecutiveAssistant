# Looksy AI vs. AI Vastra — Pricing & Licensing for a Small Independent Saree Retailer

## What pricing models does Looksy AI offer, and what do these cost?

### Takeaway
Looksy AI is a Dubai-registered (Looksy Tech FZCO), San-Francisco-marketed, Shopify-first online virtual try-on + AI shopping-assistant widget, priced as a monthly SaaS subscription in USD with credit-based usage tiers; it has no published in-store kiosk/tablet product, and its own site routes larger/custom needs to a quote request rather than a published enterprise price.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify App Store lists four tiers: **Free** ($0, unlimited try-ons listed but Looksy-branded, capped at ~$100 in attributable additional revenue), **Starter** ($14.99/month, 100 photo try-on credits, extra credits $0.14 each, up to $500 additional revenue, branding removed), **Growth** ($29/month, 300 credits, extra credits $0.12 each, up to $1,000 additional revenue), **Scale** ($79/month, 600 photo-or-video credits, extra credits $0.10 each, unlimited additional revenue, adds realtime video try-on) — [Shopify App Store: Looksy AI Virtual Try-On](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy)
- A separate web search pass against the same/adjacent listing returned different numbers — Starter $29/mo (200 credits), Growth $79/mo (600 credits), Scale $169/mo (1,500 credits), annual billing saves 17%, free tier 25 credits — indicating the live listing's pricing has changed over time or varies by locale/currency snapshot; the two sets of figures do not reconcile — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksyai); [WebSearch result, no fixed URL]
- A third-party directory (Pick Your App) shows yet another, lower set of figures (Starter $14.99/mo·100 credits in one pull; elsewhere Starter $14.99, Growth $29, Scale $79 — consistent with the first Shopify pull above) — [pickyourapp.com](https://pickyourapp.com/collections/store-design-product-display/products/looksy)
- Billing is recurring every 30 days, in USD only; no INR pricing is shown — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy)
- Looksy's own "About Us" page states pricing is **not published** and instead depends on the store's monthly try-on volume, with prospects asked to request a quote — "Pricing depends on your monthly try-on volume" — [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- Looksy's own materials describe the product purely as a web widget embedded in an online store ("responsive... works across devices and screen sizes") with **no mention of kiosks, tablets, or physical in-store hardware** — [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- Looksy's target customer description is "fashion brands selling clothing, eyewear, jewelry, or bags online" — no saree/ethnic-wear-specific mention, no India-specific mention — [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- Company background: founders Kirill Pegov and Natalia Varga; legal entity LOOKSY TECH FZCO, license No. 79748, registered at IFZA Properties, Dubai Silicon Oasis, UAE; claims up to 21% conversion increase, 3x more repeat visits, up to 10% fewer returns (vendor-reported, not independently verified) — [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- Shopify listing shows the developer as "Looksy," San Francisco, CA, US, app launched December 12, 2025, rated 3.7/5 from 9 reviews — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy)

### Inferences
- Looksy AI is architecturally an **e-commerce (online storefront) add-on**, not an in-store tablet/kiosk product. If Ma Lakhsmi Stores' Q4 2026 goal of "2 tablets mounted in the Premium Lounge" is meant literally, Looksy AI as currently documented does not appear to be a fit unless a custom/enterprise kiosk SKU exists that isn't publicly listed (its own site funnels volume/custom needs to "contact us," so this can't be ruled out, only that it's undocumented publicly).
- The entry-level paid tier ($14.99–$29/month, roughly ₹1,250–₹2,500/month at ~₹83–85/USD) is trivially affordable against a ₹1.2–1.5 lakh/month revenue base, but it buys only 100–300 online-store photo try-on credits/month — sized for a Shopify storefront's product-page traffic, not a physical kiosk serving walk-in customers.
- The inconsistency between the two Shopify pricing pulls (Starter $14.99 vs. $29) suggests the plan has been repriced recently or A/B tested; treat current published numbers as directionally right but verify at checkout before committing.

### Gaps
- No confirmed kiosk/tablet SKU, hardware bundle, or setup fee for Looksy AI — its site redirects bespoke/volume pricing to a sales quote that this research could not obtain.
- No India-specific pricing or INR billing option found for Looksy AI; all public pricing is USD-only.
- No case studies or reviews from small independent retailers (vs. larger brands) were found for Looksy AI within the 2025–2026 window.
- Could not determine number of devices/stores supported per plan, or any per-SKU/garment digitization limits — not stated on any page reviewed.

## What pricing models does AI Vastra offer, and what do these cost?

### Takeaway
AI Vastra is an India-based (Surat, Gujarat) product from Nice Digitals, explicitly marketed to Indian fashion/saree retailers and offering a **prepaid, INR-denominated credit-pack model with no recurring subscription**, plus an explicitly-named (but pricing-undisclosed) "Retail Kiosk Solutions" line for physical stores — making it directionally the more India/saree/small-business-fit product of the two, though kiosk hardware/pricing specifics are not public.

### Cited Findings
- AI Vastra's own pricing page (aivastra.com/pricing) lists prepaid, non-expiring credit packs in INR, with **no monthly subscription**:
  - **AI Virtual Try-On plans:** Starter Pack ₹1,000 → 160 try-ons (~₹6.25/try-on), positioned "Perfect for Startups"; Growth Pack ₹2,500 → 450 try-ons (~₹5.56/try-on), "Most Popular Choice"; Pro Pack ₹5,000 → 960 try-ons (~₹5.25/try-on), "Best for Growing Businesses"; Enterprise Pack ₹10,000 → 2,000 try-ons (~₹5.00/try-on), "Enterprises & High Volume." All include instant priority processing, white-label integration, website + Shopify integration, standard AI quality — [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
  - **AI Catalogue plans** (separate from try-on): Starter ₹1,000 → 80 images/8 videos; Growth ₹2,500 → 225 images/20 videos; Pro ₹5,000 → 480 images/50 videos; Enterprise ₹10,000 → 1,000 images/100 videos. All include standard AI models, standard backgrounds, catalogue templates, email support — [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
  - The page states "Pay Only for Successful Try-Ons" and failed try-ons do not deduct credits — [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
- The Shopify App Store independently lists AI Vastra as **one-time charge** tiers in USD: Free (25 credits/5 try-ons, no card required), Silver $10 (800 credits/160 try-ons), Gold $25 (2,250 credits/450 try-ons), Platinum $100 (10,000 credits/2,000 try-ons) — these figures match the INR pricing 1:~100 ratio (e.g., $10 ≈ ₹1,000 for the same 160 try-ons), suggesting the USD Shopify listing is simply the INR pricing re-denominated, not a separate schedule — [Shopify App Store: AI Vastra](https://apps.shopify.com/aivastra)
- Shopify listing credits the developer as "Nice Interactive," based in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh, India (note: this differs from the "Nice Digitals, Surat, Gujarat" attribution found via a job posting — see discrepancy below); app launched August 24, 2026, with 0 reviews — [Shopify App Store: AI Vastra](https://apps.shopify.com/aivastra)
- A job posting for a Product Marketing Executive role describes the company (there called "Nice Digitals," Surat, Gujarat) as offering three product lines: "realistic AI Virtual Try-On experiences," "AI-generated catalogues," and "retail kiosk solutions" — aimed at garment manufacturers, brands, retailers, and e-commerce businesses — [Weekday job posting: AI Vastra](https://jobs.weekday.works/product-marketing-executive-at-ai-vastra-wkdyhzl2g0)
- AI Vastra's own homepage confirms the kiosk use case directly: "Virtual try-on can run in offline stores through a kiosk or tablet setup, in a store or shopping mall," and lists supported channels as Shopify app, website integration, and kiosks/tablets (plus a Google Play listing) — but gives **no kiosk pricing, hardware specs, or setup fee** — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com)
- AI Vastra's homepage explicitly lists supported garment categories including **sarees, lehengas, kurtas, bridal wear, men's, children's, and accessories** — the only one of the two products with an explicit saree-category claim — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com)
- The homepage targets "Indian fashion brands, clothing retailers, and e-commerce sellers" without drawing an explicit small-vs-enterprise size threshold beyond the pack names (Starter/Growth/Pro/Enterprise) — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com)
- The site cites traditional photoshoot costs of ₹15,000–₹50,000+ per shoot as the comparison point for its AI catalogue pricing (a vendor framing, not independently verified) — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com)
- Contact details given: web form, email, and phone +91 7729883692; free demo offered as a "live walkthrough with the team" — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com)
- No published case study or review of an AI Vastra kiosk deployed in a saree store was found; the closest adjacent material is (a) a 2025 academic paper (Birmingham City University) prototyping a smart-mirror AR saree try-on system for stores lacking fitting rooms (a research prototype, not a commercial AI Vastra deployment), and (b) Nalli Silks' case study with **Syte** (a different, unrelated vendor) reporting a 5.7x conversion lift — not evidence for AI Vastra — [BCU paper](https://www.open-access.bcu.ac.uk/17060/1/JAIT-V16N3-426.pdf); [Syte/Nalli Silks case study](https://www.syte.ai/case-studies/product-discovery/nalli-silks/)

### Inferences
- AI Vastra is a materially better fit on paper for Ma Lakhsmi Stores than Looksy AI: it is India-based, INR-priced, explicitly lists sarees as a supported category, and explicitly names a "Retail Kiosk Solutions" product aligned with the goal of in-store tablets — but the kiosk product's actual price, hardware requirements, and per-device licensing are **not publicly disclosed**, so a sales conversation/demo request would be required before this can be budgeted.
- At current published pricing, even the Enterprise try-on pack (₹10,000 for 2,000 try-ons, ~₹5/try-on) is cheap relative to a ₹1.2–1.5 lakh/month revenue base — the credit-pack (not subscription) model also suits a seasonal/low-initial-footfall store better than a recurring SaaS fee, since unused credits reportedly don't expire.
- The discrepancy between "Nice Interactive (Rajahmundry, AP)" on the Shopify listing and "Nice Digitals (Surat, Gujarat)" on the job posting raises a flag: these could be a parent/subsidiary or reseller relationship, a rebrand, or two distinct entities incorrectly conflated by search results — this should be verified directly (e.g., on a demo call) before signing anything, especially since the Shopify app itself shows a very recent launch date (Aug 2026) and zero reviews.
- The AI Catalogue product (photo/video generation for product listings) is a plausible near-term use even before any kiosk purchase, since Ma Lakhsmi Stores will need digital catalogue imagery for its Instagram/WhatsApp funnel regardless of try-on hardware decisions — and at ₹1,000–₹2,500 entry packs this is low-risk to pilot.

### Gaps
- No public price, hardware spec sheet, per-device licensing fee, or setup fee exists for AI Vastra's "Retail Kiosk Solutions" — the one product most relevant to the stated Q4 2026 goal (2 tablets in a Premium Lounge). This is the single biggest pricing gap in this research and would need a direct sales/demo inquiry (contact: aivastra.com form, or +91 7729883692) to close.
- Could not confirm whether the kiosk product requires AI Vastra's own tablet hardware, a license to run on owner-supplied tablets, or a hybrid; the homepage only says it "can run... through a kiosk or tablet setup."
- Could not independently verify the "Nice Interactive" vs. "Nice Digitals" company-identity discrepancy.
- No case studies, reviews, or references from any small independent retailer (saree or otherwise) using AI Vastra's try-on, catalogue, or kiosk products were found.

## Are there small-business/startup tiers vs. enterprise-only pricing, for either product?

### Takeaway
Both products publish small-business-labeled entry tiers rather than being enterprise-gated for their core (online) try-on features, but neither discloses a small-business price specifically for in-store kiosk hardware — Looksy has no kiosk product at all, and AI Vastra's kiosk tier pricing is undisclosed regardless of business size.

### Cited Findings
- Looksy AI: entry paid tier is $14.99/month (or $29/month per the other data pull), explicitly the lowest of a four-tier ladder (Free/Starter/Growth/Scale) — no enterprise-only gate for the core online try-on widget, but larger/custom volume is routed to a sales quote on the company's own site — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy); [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- AI Vastra: explicitly labels its ₹1,000 Starter Pack "Perfect for Small Businesses" / "Perfect for Startups," and its ₹10,000 top pack "Enterprises & High Volume" — a clear small-business-first framing for the online try-on and catalogue products — [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
- Neither product's public pricing page lists a specific price tier for the physical kiosk/tablet deployment at any business size — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com); [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)

### Inferences
- For the online/Shopify try-on feature alone, cost is a non-issue for Ma Lakhsmi Stores at either vendor — both are priced for solo/small merchants.
- The actual budget risk sits entirely in the undisclosed kiosk pricing, which is likely sold as a custom/sales-assisted deal (common for hardware+software bundles) rather than a self-serve tier — consistent with Looksy's volume-based "contact us" pattern and AI Vastra's silence on kiosk pricing specifically.

### Gaps
- Whether AI Vastra's kiosk solution has a "small business" vs. "enterprise" price distinction is unknown and was not found in any public source.

## What's included vs. extra (SKUs/garments digitized, devices, support, updates)?

### Takeaway
Both vendors gate their plans primarily on try-on/credit volume rather than SKU count or device count; device/kiosk count, update cadence, and dedicated support tiers are undocumented for both, and SKU/garment limits are explicitly "unlimited" only for Looksy's paid tiers and implicitly unlimited (no stated cap) for AI Vastra.

### Cited Findings
- Looksy paid tiers (Starter/Growth/Scale) all include "unlimited size, bundles, and email capture" and remove Looksy branding; the Free tier keeps branding and caps attributable revenue at $100 — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy)
- Looksy's Scale tier ($79/month in one pricing pull, $169/month in another) is the only tier with realtime video try-on (vs. photo-only on lower tiers) — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy); [WebSearch result]
- AI Vastra's try-on plans all include "try-on history, multiple garment categories, and unlimited products" even at the lowest ($10/₹1,000) Silver/Starter tier — [Shopify App Store: AI Vastra](https://apps.shopify.com/aivastra); [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
- AI Vastra's catalogue plans include email support at every tier; its try-on plans do not list support as an included line item — [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
- Neither vendor's public pages specify a number-of-devices or number-of-stores limit per plan — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy); [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)
- Neither vendor's public pages mention update cadence, SLA, or a named support tier beyond "chat support" (Looksy) or "email support" (AI Vastra catalogue only) — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy); [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- For a single-store operation like Ma Lakhsmi Stores, the lack of a device/store cap on either plan is favorable — cost scales with try-on volume, not with the number of tablets deployed, assuming the kiosk licensing (once disclosed) follows the same logic.

### Gaps
- No information on update/maintenance cadence, uptime SLA, or escalation support for either product beyond generic "chat"/"email" mentions.

## Are there any known India-specific pricing or currency considerations?

### Takeaway
AI Vastra prices natively and exclusively in INR with India-specific framing (cost comparison to Indian photoshoot rates, saree/lehenga/kurta category support, Surat/Gujarat base), while Looksy AI publishes USD-only pricing with no India-specific plan, currency option, or GST/tax note found on either vendor's pages.

### Cited Findings
- AI Vastra's pricing page is denominated entirely in INR with no USD option shown, and frames its catalogue value against "traditional photoshoot costs of ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 or more per shoot" — an India-market-specific comparison — [aivastra.com/pricing](https://aivastra.com)
- AI Vastra is based in Surat, Gujarat (per job posting) or Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh (per Shopify developer listing) — both Indian locations, reinforcing an India-first go-to-market — [Weekday job posting](https://jobs.weekday.works/product-marketing-executive-at-ai-vastra-wkdyhzl2g0); [Shopify App Store: AI Vastra](https://apps.shopify.com/aivastra)
- Looksy AI's Shopify listing states "all charges are billed in USD," with no INR toggle found, and the company is UAE-registered (Dubai Silicon Oasis) with no India-specific mention anywhere in its own materials — [Shopify App Store: Looksy](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy); [Looksy — About Us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- No GST treatment, import/customs consideration (for any physical kiosk hardware), or India payment-method (UPI, etc.) detail was found for either vendor on the pages reviewed.

### Inferences
- For a Jalpaiguri-based retailer, AI Vastra's INR-native pricing and explicit saree-category support removes currency-conversion/forex friction and signals a product built with the Indian saree/ethnic-wear retail context in mind — a meaningfully better directional fit than Looksy AI, which shows no awareness of the Indian ethnic-wear market in its own public materials.
- Paying Looksy in USD would add minor forex-conversion overhead (card foreign-transaction fees, INR/USD rate exposure) on top of the already-modest subscription cost — immaterial at this revenue scale but worth noting for bookkeeping.

### Gaps
- No GST invoicing, UPI/India payment gateway, or import-duty (for kiosk hardware, if hardware is sold/shipped rather than licensed onto owner-supplied tablets) information was found for either product.

# Looksy AI vs. AI Vastra — Company Background, India Presence, Support Track Record

## Important framing note / major risk signal

Both product names are ambiguous and resolve to multiple, unrelated entities online. This naming collision is itself a vendor-risk finding: a small single-store buyer doing due diligence by name alone is likely to get confused or contact the wrong vendor.

- **"Looksy"** resolves to at least two distinct, seemingly unrelated businesses: (1) **Looksy (looksy.tech)**, a Dubai-registered company ("LOOKSY TECH FZCO"), and (2) **"Looksy AI Virtual Try‑On,"** a Shopify App Store listing whose developer address is given as San Francisco, launched December 12, 2025. No source found ties these two together, and no source confirms they are the same company. — [looksy.tech/about_us](https://looksy.tech/about_us); [Shopify Looksy listing](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy)
- **"AI Vastra"** resolves to **aivastra.com**, described on its own site as "a product by Nice Digitals," with a corporate office listed in Hyderabad and a head office listed in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh — but a separate recruiting listing for the same product describes it as Surat, Gujarat-based. These are conflicting location claims from different first-party-adjacent sources. — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com); [Weekday job listing](https://jobs.weekday.works/product-marketing-executive-at-ai-vastra-wkdyhzl2g0)
- Neither "Looksy" nor "AI Vastra" turned up in India retail-tech press (Economic Times Retail, YourStory, Inc42, Entrackr) in any search performed. Coverage of Indian virtual try-on vendors instead surfaces **other** named companies (AlphaBake, Aaiena, TryRobe, mirrAR) — none of which are the two products in scope. This absence of trade-press coverage for both target vendors is itself notable and should be flagged to the user.

## Is there any indication of which product is better suited to a small single-store client versus large multi-store chains?

### Takeaway
Neither vendor publishes pricing or packaging explicitly targeted at small, single-outlet physical retailers; both products as documented are built around online/e-commerce try-on widgets (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento) rather than the tablet-based in-store kiosk use case Soumyanil's plan envisions, which is itself an important mismatch to flag.

### Cited Findings
- Looksy's documented product (both the looksy.tech widget and the Shopify app) is explicitly a **website embed** for online stores — "the try-on works within the store interface" and "does not require users to leave the website" — with no in-store tablet/kiosk deployment mentioned anywhere on its own site or in any third-party source found. — [looksy.tech/about_us](https://looksy.tech/about_us)
- Looksy's Shopify pricing is tiered by monthly try-on credit volume (Free; Starter $14.99/mo for 100 credits; Growth $29/mo for 300 credits; Scale $79/mo for 600 credits with video try-on) — structured for an online store with variable traffic, not a flat per-tablet in-store fee. — [Shopify Looksy listing](https://apps.shopify.com/looksy)
- AI Vastra's own site explicitly lists "Retail Kiosk Solutions" alongside AI virtual try-on and AI catalogue generation as one of its three core offerings, targeting "garment manufacturers, brands, retailers, boutiques, and e-commerce sellers" — this is the only one of the two vendors whose stated product line explicitly includes an in-store kiosk/tablet-style offering matching Soumyanil's plan. — [aivastra.com](https://aivastra.com); [Weekday job listing](https://jobs.weekday.works/product-marketing-executive-at-ai-vastra-wkdyhzl2g0)

### Inferences
- Based on stated product scope alone, **AI Vastra is the more plausible fit** for Soumyanil's actual plan (a single physical outlet wanting tablet-based in-store try-on), since it is the only one of the two that explicitly advertises a retail kiosk product; Looksy's entire documented offering is an e-commerce website widget, which would not serve an in-store tablet use case at all unless Looksy has an undocumented hardware/kiosk product not found in this research.
- This suggests Soumyanil may be comparing two products that aren't actually equivalent for his use case — worth confirming directly with both vendors (does Looksy even offer in-store hardware/tablet deployment at all?) before treating this as an apples-to-apples choice.

### Gaps

- No source confirms whether Looksy has any in-store/tablet/kiosk product line at all — this should be asked directly of the vendor, since everything found describes only a website widget.

## Current Competitor Snapshots

### National Textile
- **Identity:** Proprietorship, Dinbazar Rd, Jalpaiguri. Self-reported ₹50L–1cr turnover, 26–50 staff (directory-only, unverified). Wholesaler + retailer hybrid.
- **Positioning:** Traditional Din Bazar cloth-market operator, no trend/brand positioning.
- **Basic features:** Full-range saree catalog, physical storefront, standard payment options, 7-day replacement window.
- **Advanced features:** Wholesaler+retailer bulk-sourcing leverage.
- **Traction:** No reviews/social/press found. Din Bazar location = inherent foot traffic.
- **Weaknesses:** Zero digital footprint anywhere.
- **Watch next:** Any sign of going digital (Instagram/WhatsApp).
- **Sources:** textileinfomedia.com/national-textile-jalpaiguri, accessed 2026-10-24.

### Td and Sons
- **Identity:** Proprietorship, Din Bazar, Jalpaiguri. Self-reported 26–50 staff vs. ≤₹10L turnover (internally inconsistent — low confidence). Wholesaler + retailer hybrid.
- **Positioning:** Generic Din Bazar wholesale-retail counter, no niche curation.
- **Basic features:** Full-range saree catalog, physical storefront, cheque/bank-transfer payment, 10-day return/exchange.
- **Advanced features:** Wholesaler+retailer bulk-sourcing leverage.
- **Traction:** No reviews/social/press found.
- **Weaknesses:** Zero digital footprint; inconsistent self-reported scale figures.
- **Watch next:** Any sign of going digital.
- **Sources:** textileinfomedia.com/td-and-sons, accessed 2026-10-24.

### Avantra by Trends
- **Identity:** Reliance Retail national chain, launched Sept 2021, 73 stores by May 2024. Siliguri location is a franchise (City Centre Mall, since ~Feb 2023).
- **Positioning:** "House of India's finest ethnic wear" — 10 in-house sub-brands spanning price/occasion tiers, Nalli/Pothys collabs, direct weaver-cluster partnerships.
- **Basic features:** Full saree/ethnic catalog, in-house blouse stitching/alteration, mall-format store, standard POS.
- **Advanced features:** Multi-sub-brand segmented curation; omnichannel (avantra.ajio.com); franchise-model national expansion; national supply chain + weaver-cluster/branded collabs.
- **Traction:** 4.5/5 from 42 reviews (Yappe) — legacy signal, reviews dated ~2022-23, no fresh 2024-26 reviews found.
- **Weaknesses:** One unverified billing-overcharge complaint; no AI try-on/WhatsApp/Instagram-native marketing found; Reliance-owned anonymity vs. local founder trust.
- **Watch next:** Whether Avantra starts localized Instagram/WhatsApp marketing in Siliguri.
- **Sources:** relianceretail.com/avantra-by-trends.html; indiaretailing.com (Feb 2024, May 2024); in.fashionnetwork.com (Sep 2021, Feb 2023); yappe.in listing. All accessed 2026-10-24.

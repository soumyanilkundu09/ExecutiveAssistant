# Team

No hires yet — these are the planned roles for Ma Lakhsmi Stores once it launches:

- **Store Manager / Lead Stylist (1)** — most critical hire; needs deep local roots in Jalpaiguri, fluent Bengali and Hindi, strong grasp of current fashion trends.
- **Retail Sales Associates (2)** — run the front "Volume Zone" (daily wear/cottons), assist the Store Manager at peak hours, handle stock organization, keep trial rooms clear.
- **Billing & Inventory Executive (1)** — tech-literate, owns the POS system.
- **Master Tailor** — local, commission/piece-rate partner for alterations and custom blouse stitching.

**Communication:** Email or WhatsApp.

**Biggest pain point:** Visibility — no one clearly knows what the others are doing. Favor status-check / shared-visibility workflows over assuming information will surface on its own.
